Pick the small swatch closest to the centre colour.

Distance is ΔEOK, straight-line distance in OKLab (the Cartesian form of OKLCH), shown ×100. A game is 10 rounds; Easy spreads the swatches wider, Hard packs them tighter, and each mode keeps its own best. Changing mode starts a new game. Closest scores 10, second 7, third 4.5, any other up to 4. Subtle calls earn a bonus: up to +5 for the closest, +3 for second, +2 for third. A call is subtle when the ΔE gap is small: between the closest and runner-up when you're right, or between your pick and the closest when you're not. Swatches get closer together as your streak grows. Keys 1–9 and 0 pick clockwise from the top; Enter moves on.
